# 06 Analytics & Reporting

Track what's working. Monthly reviews of sales pipeline, marketing performance, and growth metrics.

## Subfolders

### Monthly Reports
- Consolidated monthly business reports
- Revenue tracking
- Client acquisition metrics

### Social Media Metrics
- Follower growth, engagement rates, reach
- Top-performing content analysis
- Platform-by-platform breakdowns

### Website Analytics
- Traffic, bounce rate, conversion tracking
- Most-visited pages and user flow
- SEO ranking progress

### Sales Metrics
- Lead-to-close conversion rates
- Average deal size and timeline
- Pipeline velocity
- Win/loss analysis

### Campaign Performance
- ROI tracking per campaign
- A/B test results
- Cost-per-lead and cost-per-acquisition data
