## Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Blog and landing page voice follows Visiominds' core voice with specific SEO adjustments:**

- **Confident authority.** Write as the expert. Not "we think branding might help your business" but "branding is the difference between a business that gets chosen and one that gets overlooked."
- **Nigerian market specificity.** Always ground advice in the Nigerian context. Reference Naira, Lagos, Nigerian consumer behavior, local market dynamics. A reader should feel this was written FOR them, not copied from an American marketing blog.
- **Search-intent alignment.** Match the tone to what the searcher wants. Someone searching "how much does branding cost in Nigeria" wants a direct answer, not a philosophical essay. Someone searching "best brand identities in Nigeria" wants visual examples and analysis.
- **No keyword stuffing.** Integrate keywords naturally. If a sentence sounds awkward because of a keyword, rewrite it.
- **Case study integration.** Weave in Visiominds' work naturally. Not "we're the best, hire us" but "when we worked with Cleaniche, the challenge was [X]. Here's how we approached it..." This builds credibility through demonstration, not claims.

## Quality Criteria

1. **Every article targets a specific keyword** with clear search intent match
2. **Every article includes 2+ internal links** to other Visiominds pages
3. **Every article has optimized title tag, meta description, and URL slug**
4. **Every image has descriptive alt text** including relevant keywords where natural
5. **Content is factually accurate** — pricing, service descriptions, and client references are current
6. **No thin content** — every page provides genuine value that could not be found by just reading the first Google result
7. **Mobile-friendly formatting** — short paragraphs (3-4 lines max on mobile), bulleted lists, clear headings, readable font sizes
8. **Page speed acceptable** — all images compressed, no render-blocking scripts, lazy loading for below-fold content
9. **No duplicate content** — every page is unique, no copy-pasted sections across articles
10. **CTA present on every page** — every blog post and landing page has a clear path to contact Visiominds

## Constraints & Rules

1. **Never publish thin or AI-generated-sounding content.** Every article must have genuine insights, specific examples, and the Visiominds perspective. If it reads like it could appear on any generic marketing blog, it is not good enough.
2. **Never engage in black-hat SEO tactics.** No keyword stuffing, no hidden text, no link schemes, no purchased backlinks from spammy sites.
3. **Always check for existing content cannibalization** before creating a new article. Two articles targeting the same keyword compete with each other.
4. **Update existing content before creating new content** on the same topic. Refreshing a 6-month-old article is often more effective than writing a new one.
5. **Blog posts must be published on visio-minds.com/blog** — not on a subdomain or external platform. Keep all SEO equity on the main domain.
6. **Never promise specific ranking results.** SEO is a long game. Set expectations around content quality and consistency, not "we will rank #1 for X."
7. **Every landing page must load in under 3 seconds** on a Nigerian 4G connection. Performance is non-negotiable for Nigerian users.
8. **Technical SEO baseline must be maintained:** XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical tags, structured data markup, HTTPS, mobile-responsive.
9. **Content must be reviewed for accuracy** before publishing. Pricing tiers, service descriptions, and client details must match current offerings.
10. **Track and report keyword rankings monthly.** Use free tools (Google Search Console) or affordable tools (Ubersuggest, SE Ranking) given the agency's budget.
