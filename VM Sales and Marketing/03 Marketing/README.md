# 03 Marketing

All marketing channels and campaigns. The goal: build brand awareness, generate inbound leads, and position Visiominds as the premium creative agency in Nigeria.

## Subfolders

### Content Calendar
- Monthly/weekly content planning docs
- Publishing schedules across all platforms
- Theme and campaign alignment

### Social Media
- **Instagram** — Primary visual platform (carousel posts, reels, stories)
- **LinkedIn** — B2B thought leadership and case study sharing
- **Twitter-X** — Quick takes, industry commentary, engagement
- **Behance** — Portfolio showcase for design community visibility
- **Templates & Graphics** — Reusable post templates, story templates

### Email Marketing
- **Newsletter Templates** — Monthly/biweekly newsletter designs
- **Drip Sequences** — Automated email flows (welcome, nurture, re-engagement)
- **Subscriber Lists** — Segment definitions and list management

### SEO & Website
- **Keyword Research** — Target keywords for the Nigerian creative market
- **Blog Strategy** — Content pillars, topic clusters, publishing cadence
- **Landing Pages** — Service-specific landing page copy and designs

### Campaigns
- Time-bound marketing campaigns (launches, promotions, seasonal)
- Campaign briefs, timelines, and asset lists

### Partnerships & Referrals
- Referral program structure
- Partnership opportunities (complementary agencies, tech companies)
- Co-marketing proposals

### Paid Ads
- **Meta Ads** — Instagram/Facebook ad campaigns
- **Google Ads** — Search and display campaigns
- **Creative Assets** — Ad visuals, copy variations, A/B test results
