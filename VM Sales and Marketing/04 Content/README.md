# 04 Content

The content engine. Case studies are Visiominds' strongest sales weapon — every project should become a story worth telling.

## Subfolders

### Case Studies
- **Published** — Final, approved case studies ready for website and pitches
- **Drafts** — Work-in-progress case studies
- **Templates** — Case study writing framework (problem → strategy → execution → results)

### Blog Posts
- **Published** — Live blog content
- **Drafts** — In-progress articles

### Portfolio Pieces
- Curated portfolio items for specific audiences or industries
- One-page portfolio samplers for cold outreach

### Video & Reels
- **Scripts** — Video scripts for social content
- **Behind The Scenes** — Process documentation and studio content
- **Process Videos** — Design process breakdowns (timelapse, before/after)

### Thought Leadership
- Industry opinion pieces and insights
- Design trend analyses
- Founder/team perspectives on branding and strategy

### Testimonials & Reviews
- Client quotes and endorsements
- Video testimonial transcripts
- Google/social media review screenshots
- Client satisfaction data (from project stats)
