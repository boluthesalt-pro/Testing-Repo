## 🎯 The 5 Principles Behind Every Task

| # | Principle | What it means for us this month |
|---|-----------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | **Relationship building is the long game** | 20 conversations may yield 1 client. That 1 matters. We play for the compounding effect, not instant wins. |
| 2 | **Have conversations, not pitches** | We reach out to founders to *talk*, not to send a deck. Low-pressure, genuine, specific. |
| 3 | **The portfolio closes the deal** | A DM opens the door; the work decides if they walk through. Our portfolio must be undeniable. |
| 4 | **Over-deliver on every project** | Treat each client as a chance to expand our craft — even beyond the brief. Track record = future referrals. |
| 5 | **Increase surface area** | Everyone we already know should know what VisioMinds does and how good we are. This is the lowest-hanging fruit. |

## 🔁 The Weekly Engine (repeats every week)

This is the rhythm. Every week follows the same five-day shape, so it becomes a habit:

| Day | Theme | Core actions |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| **Monday** | 🎯 Plan & Prep | Pick the week's 10 prospects, research them, decide the week's post |
| **Tuesday** | 📨 Outreach Day 1 | Each partner sends 5 personalised DMs. Log every one. |
| **Wednesday** | 🎨 Content Build | Build the week's post (carousel + caption) from a case study |
| **Thursday** | 💬 Conversations + Publish | Reply to leads, push toward calls; publish post to IG + LinkedIn |
| **Friday** | 🔄 Follow-up & Review | Follow up non-responders, update pipeline, weekly review |
| **Sat/Sun** | 😌 Light | Optional: like/comment on prospects' posts. No obligations. |

**Posting cadence:** ONE anchor post per week, adapted for both Instagram (carousel) and LinkedIn (text + images). Published Thursday. That's it — no daily posting.

# 🧰 The Playbook (reference)

## ✉️ Outreach DM template — conversation, not pitch
> Personalise the **bold** parts every time. Never send a deck first.

```
Hi [Name] — I've followed [Brand] for a while and genuinely rate
**[specific thing you admire]**. I run VisioMinds, a brand & design
studio here in Lagos — we recently rebranded **[relevant client, e.g.
Cleaniche]**.

Not pitching anything. I just think there's real room to make [Brand]'s
brand identity match the quality of the **[product / work]** itself.
Happy to share a couple of quick ideas if you're open — either way,
keep doing what you're doing 🔥
```
*(Full vertical-specific versions: `02 Sales/Proposals & Pitch Decks/outreach-templates-by-vertical.pdf`)*
