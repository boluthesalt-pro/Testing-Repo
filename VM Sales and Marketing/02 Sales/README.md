# 02 Sales

Everything needed to find, pitch, and close clients. From cold outreach to signed contracts.

## Subfolders

### Lead Generation
- **Prospecting Lists** — Target companies by industry, size, and need
- **Outreach Templates** — Email and LinkedIn message templates
- **Cold DM Scripts** — Instagram and WhatsApp outreach scripts (Nigerian market context)

### Proposals & Pitch Decks
- **Templates** — Reusable proposal structures for each service type
- **Client Proposals** — Customized proposals for specific prospects

### Pricing & Packages
- Service pricing sheets (Starter, Standard, Premium, Enterprise)
- Package bundles and upsell combinations
- Pricing comparison sheets for client conversations

### Discovery Calls
- **Scripts** — Structured call frameworks for qualifying leads
- **Prep Sheets** — Pre-call research templates
- **Follow-Up Templates** — Post-call emails, WhatsApp messages, and next-step prompts

### Pipeline Tracking
- Lead status tracking (enquiry → discovery call → proposal → negotiation → closed)
- Win/loss analysis templates
- Monthly pipeline reviews

### Objection Handling
- Common client objections and response frameworks
- Price objection rebuttals
- "Why Visiominds over competitors" talking points
