# 01 Brand Foundation

The source of truth for Visiominds' brand identity, voice, and market positioning. Every piece of sales and marketing material should reference these docs.

## Subfolders

### Brand Guidelines
- Visual identity rules (colors, typography, logo usage)
- Brand book / style guide
- Do's and don'ts for brand representation

### Brand Assets
- Logo files (all formats: PNG, SVG, light/dark variants)
- Color palette exports
- Typography specimens
- Social media profile images and banners
- Email signatures

### Messaging Framework
- Brand voice and tone guide
- Taglines and elevator pitches
- Service descriptions (short, medium, long versions)
- Boilerplate "About Visiominds" copy
- Key messages per audience segment

### Competitor Analysis
- Nigerian creative agency landscape
- Direct competitor profiles (services, pricing, positioning)
- Differentiators and gap analysis
- Market trends and opportunities
