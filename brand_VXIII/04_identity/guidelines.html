Introduction

About these guidelines

This document sets out how VXIII looks, speaks and presents itself. It applies to the product, the packaging, print, the website and every message that carries the name.

The identity rests on one idea. A person is worth what they keep of themselves, and a frame should help them keep it. The rules are few so that they are kept.

Version 2.0 follows the approved brand story, research, mood board, constructions and colourways. The logo is unchanged. The two models are now Halite and Fleur, and the brand palette has new names so that no brand colour shares a name with a product.

Brand foundation

The name

VXIII is one word and one reference, Matthew 5:13. It is written and read together, with no space and no mark between the letters.

The V stands for the chapter and the XIII for the verse, so a single word carries the whole citation.

The verse opens with "Ye are the salt of the earth" (KJV) and asks what happens when salt has lost its savour. The next verse, 5:14, reads "Ye are the light of the world." VXIII sits one verse before the light. The wearer is the salt, and the frame is what they wear into the light.

VXIII · BRAND IDENTITY GUIDELINES07

Why salt, and why the sun

Made by the sun

Sea water is let into shallow pans and left under hard light until only crystal remains. The light that makes salt is the light a pair of sunglasses is built to face.

It preserves

Scripture calls a lasting promise "a covenant of salt" (Numbers 18:19). A frame bought once and kept for years follows the same logic.

Noticed by its absence

Nobody sees salt in a meal, and people notice it only when it is missing. VXIII is worn the same way.

It has a measure

The Latin salarium, from which salary comes, is built on sal, the Latin for salt. "Worth your salt" still means someone has earned their place.

Manifesto

You are the salt of the earth.
A little salt changes everything it is added to.
The sun that dries the pans is the light you walk into. Meet it with something that holds its edge.
Keep your savour.

VXIII · BRAND IDENTITY GUIDELINES08

Audience and personality

Who we speak to

People who buy few things and keep them. They value a good material and a clean line, and they choose an object for how it is made before they look at the name on it.

They come to us through a friend or through the verse itself. Most already own sunglasses they have lost or outgrown, and they want one pair that stays. We write to them as equals, with the plain confidence of someone who has nothing to prove.

Logo

The wordmark

The wordmark is the primary signature. It is a solid, heavy drawing, cut square at the cap and base lines, with a V that narrows toward its point and an X that crosses at the centre. It reads as one piece, the way the verse reads as one reference.

It is supplied as vector artwork in Ink, in Salt White and on each approved ground. Use these files and do not retype it in a font.

Typography

Two typefaces

KEEP YOUR SAVOUR

Drawn for the brand as a single light stroke, with flat cuts at the cap and base lines and capitals only. It sits beside the heavy wordmark as its quiet companion.

Hanken Grotesk

TEXT, CAPTIONS AND SPECIFICATIONS
Keep your savour. Built on titanium and drawn from salt.

A clear, even grotesque with an open, upright shape, used for every line of running text and for captions in small capitals. Weights 300, 400, 500 and 600. It is free for commercial use under the SIL Open Font Licence.

Construction

Cap height 100 units, a single stroke of 11 units and a gap of 14 units between letters. Every terminal is cut flat to the cap and base lines, and round forms are drawn as even arcs.

Use

Headlines, section numbers, product names and page numbers. It is set at a tracking of 7 percent and never below 8 points.

Character set

Capitals and numerals with a short set of marks. Lower case letters set in the face draw as capitals. The font file is supplied with the guidelines.

VXIII · BRAND IDENTITY GUIDELINES30

Setting type

Do

Set text flush left and leave the right edge ragged.
Use VXIII Display for headlines and numerals and Hanken Grotesk for everything that is read in a line.
Set captions in small capitals with 14 percent tracking.
Keep a measure of 45 to 75 characters for body text.
Leave generous room around a headline.

Avoid

Justified text, centred paragraphs and italics.
Setting body text in VXIII Display.
Adding weights to VXIII Display, which is drawn in one weight.
Setting the wordmark in any font. The wordmark is always the supplied drawing.
Type set over a pattern without a clear field.

VXIII · BRAND IDENTITY GUIDELINES34

Signature details

HALITE, THE SALT LINE
The lens edge bevelled at 45 degrees and polished, so a white line runs round each lens like salt at the edge of a drying pan.
FLEUR, THE HOPPER SQUARE
Three squares stepped one inside another, from the hopper crystal of fleur de sel. It sits at the lifted corner of the frame and at the end of the temple.

These two details identify each model without a logo on the front. They may be drawn as single line graphics in Ink or Gold for print, at no less than 6 mm across.

VXIII · BRAND IDENTITY GUIDELINES35

The engraved mark

FIRST AREA, INNER FACE OF THE LEFT TEMPLE. VXIII IN THE WORDMARK, THE FIRST LINE OF THE VERSE, THEN THE SIZE MARK SMALL AND APART.
SECOND AREA, INNER FACE OF THE RIGHT TEMPLE. KEEP YOUR SAVOUR, ALONE.
POSITION
Inner face of each temple, where only the wearer sees it. Drawn at about 3:1.
CONTENT
The reference on the left temple and the tagline on the right, so the two temples read as the verse and its answer.
SIZE
Wordmark at a cap height of 2 mm on Halite and 1.8 mm on the narrower Fleur temple. The tagline alone, set a little larger than the verse line.
DEPTH
0.14 mm by laser, through the finish, with no filler, so the cut shows bare titanium.
