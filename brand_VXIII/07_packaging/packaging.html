The paper cover

The box travels inside a strong paper cover, a rigid sleeve open at both ends, and is pulled out of it. The cover takes the knocks of the journey in place of a shipping carton. It stays plain, so the first thing seen is quiet, the way salt is noticed only by its absence. A paper seal closes one end until the box is opened.

The unboxing sequence

The box is opened in six movements. The plain paper cover comes first, and each movement after it reads as a stage in the making of salt: the crust, the cube, the words, the product and the savour.

1The paper cover

The seal is torn and the box is pulled out of its plain paper cover.

STAGE: THE QUIET

2The box top

A Pan Blue box carrying the salt crack pattern, tone on tone, and a small gold wordmark.

STAGE: THE CRUST

3The inner lid

The hinged lid opens and the blue inner lid shows the model mark in gold.

STAGE: THE CUBE

4The notes

The inner lid lifts and the notes, the care card and the cloth lie in the first layer.

STAGE: THE WORDS

5The frame

The first layer lifts out on a ribbon and the case lies alone in the second layer.

STAGE: THE PRODUCT

6The case

The case opens at its gold plate and the frame lifts out.

STAGE: THE SAVOUR

VXIII · PACKAGING10

The box top

The cover carries the salt crack pattern, the crust of the Salar de Uyuni as it dries, drawn in single fine lines. It is debossed tone on tone so that it is felt before it is seen, and it runs unbroken across the whole face. The wordmark sits directly on it in matt gold, small, with nothing framed or cleared for it, so it does not shout.
