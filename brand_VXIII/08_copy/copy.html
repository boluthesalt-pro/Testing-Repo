One verse before the light

VXIII is one word and one reference, Matthew 5:13. The verse says you are the salt of the earth, and the next verse says you are the light of the world. We make the frame you wear into it.

Two models, eight salts

Halite is made for men and Fleur for women. Each comes in four colourways, and each colourway is named for a salt that exists.

02The two models

Halite
MEN · RIMLESS · 57 □ 19 145

Rock salt, the cube cut from the earth. A rimless frame whose lens edge is bevelled and polished, so a fine white line runs round each lens like salt at the edge of a drying pan. Titanium appears only in small cube mounts, the bridge and the temples.

Four colourways: Lava, Brine, Smoke and Uyuni.

Fleur
WOMEN · CAT EYE · 56 □ 17 140

Fleur de sel, the first and finest crystals skimmed from the surface of the pan. A cat eye in fine titanium wire whose lifted corner carries the stepped square of the hopper crystal, repeated at the end of each temple.

Four colourways: Guérande, Alaea, Retba and Kala.

06Care

Titanium does not rust and it keeps its shape. A little attention keeps the finish as it was on the day it arrived.

StepCopy
RinseRinse the frame in clean, cool water after salt water, sweat or sand, and dry it with the cloth supplied.
Clean the lensesBreathe on the lens and wipe it with the cloth, using no pressure. Use a drop of mild soap for marks that remain.
StoreFold the temples and close the frame in its case, lenses toward the lining. Keep it away from the dashboard of a car.
AvoidSolvents, hot water and paper towels. Each of them can mark a lens or a finish.
NoteCopy
HaliteHalite is rimless. Hold it by the temples or the mounts, never by the lens, and put it down with the lenses facing up.
FleurHold Fleur by the temples. The stepped corner is polished sharp, and a soft cloth keeps its edges bright.
Acetate endsHalite Smoke and Fleur Alaea finish in acetate. An optician can warm and set these ends to the shape of your ear.
Adjusting the fitTitanium can be adjusted by an optician. Ask for the nose pads and the temples to be set to your face, without heat on the titanium, since heat can mark the colour.
The finishesPolished finishes show fine marks in strong light, and these soften with wear. Brushed and matte finishes keep a uniform surface.

08The Covenant

Scripture calls a lasting promise a covenant of salt (Numbers 18:19). This is what we promise, in writing.

Clause I. Built on titanium

Titanium carries the structure of every frame, for men and for women. It carries almost no weight, does not rust and holds its shape through every season.

Clause II. Full protection

Every lens is polarised and blocks ultraviolet light up to 400 nm, on every frame and at every price.

Clause III. Genuine

Every frame is engraved inside the left temple with the wordmark, the verse and the size, and inside the right temple with Keep your savour. Every box carries a certificate of authenticity numbered and signed by hand.

Clause IV. Fourteen days to decide

If the frame is not yours, return it within fourteen days of delivery, unworn and in its box with the notes, the case and the cloth. We refund the price of the frame once it reaches us. Returns for a reason other than a defect are made at the buyer's cost.

Clause V. Twelve months of warranty

Every frame is covered for twelve months from delivery against defects in materials and making. The warranty does not extend to loss, accidental damage, scratches that come from wear, or alterations made by anyone but us.

Clause VI. Repair and adjustment

We adjust the fit of any frame at no charge for as long as you keep it. Once the warranty has ended, we repair what can be repaired at a fair price.

Clause VII. Delivery

Orders ship within three working days with tracked delivery. Duties and taxes, where they apply, are shown at checkout and are paid by the buyer.

Clause VIII. Honest dealing

We state what a frame is made of and what it does. If we make a mistake with your order, we correct it and we say so.
